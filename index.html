Across Two Courts

by Laia Albàs Ferrer

Across Two Courts captures the heart and soul of a young basketball player’s journey from Catalonia to the United States as she follows her dream to compete successfully in the game she loves.

With honesty and humor, Laia Albàs Ferrer shares the challenges of leaving home, adapting to a new culture and competing in American college basketball. Her true-life story is about courage, resilience and pursuing a dream far from home.
